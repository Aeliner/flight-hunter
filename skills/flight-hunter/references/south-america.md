# South America notes (Uruguay / Argentina / Brazil origins)

Read this when the trip starts or ends in the Southern Cone. Re-check anything time-sensitive, especially visas and taxes.

## Airports and ground links
- **Montevideo (MVD).** Few long-haul options: LATAM via SCL/LIM/GRU, Copa via PTY, AA nonstop to MIA, Iberia/Air Europa to MAD. Always compare with Buenos Aires and Brazil gateways.
- **Buenos Aires: EZE (international) and AEP (city, regional plus some international).** They're about 1h apart by road, so an AEP arrival with an EZE departure is an airport change. Comma-search `EZE,AEP`; Kayak's `BUE` covers both.
- **Ferry from Montevideo/Colonia to Buenos Aires:** Buquebus, Colonia Express, Cacciola. See sites.md. It only pays when BA's fare is much cheaper or the group meets there anyway.
- **Brazil gateways:** GRU (São Paulo) and GIG (Rio) often have far cheaper long-haul fares, especially to North America and Asia (AA, Air Canada, Qatar, Emirates, Turkish). Positioning: Gol/LATAM MVD→GRU nonstop (~$235–265 one-way), Sky MVD↔GIG (~$252 round trip). Kiwi misses some of these nonstops, so use fli.
- **Panama (PTY), Copa's hub:** cheap 1-stops from MVD/EZE/MDZ/ROS to many mid-size US cities (AUS, MCO, TPA, BOS, IAD...) and the Caribbean. Copa sometimes routes EZE→ROS→PTY. Economy Basic has no checked bag; Classic includes one.
- **Santiago (SCL), LATAM's hub:** LATAM to the US/Europe/Oceania, Sky's LCC network. The LATAM lounge there is available to LATAM Pass Gold+.
- **Lima (LIM), Bogotá (BOG):** LATAM and Avianca connections north. Lima has many overnight connections.
- **Mendoza (MDZ), Córdoba (COR), Rosario (ROS):** occasionally Copa/LATAM fares match Buenos Aires. A Mendoza start was tested and didn't pay.

## Passports and visas (Uruguayan / Argentine / Brazilian)
- **US:** none of the three is in the Visa Waiver Program, so a US visa is needed even for an airside connection. No visa means exclude US connections (Kiwi `exclude_stopover_countries: "US"`; fli `--exclude-airlines` won't do it). Use routings via PTY/BOG/MEX→Europe/Middle East instead.
- **Canada:** eTA only with a valid US visa or a Canadian visa in the last 10 years (by air). Otherwise a visa.
- **Panama (Copa's PTY hub):** airside transit is normal for Copa connections and generally needs no visa for these passports. Confirm the current rules if anyone's passport is unusual, or if a layover means leaving the airport.
- **Mexico:** visa-free for these passports as tourists, but check the current rules. Connections in MEX can trigger entry checks.
- **Schengen:** visa-free 90 days. **UK:** airside transit generally visa-free for these nationalities. **Qatar/UAE:** visa-free or on-arrival. A Middle East routing is a no-US-visa option to Asia.
- **Japan:** 90 days visa-free. Uruguayan passports issued April–August 2025 lacked a place of birth, and Japan warned about refusals. Check the issue date.
- **Asia transit and hubs** (Uruguayan passport, as found Nov 2026; re-check):
  - Korea: 90 days visa-free, but a **K-ETA** is required (KRW 10,000, ≥72h ahead).
  - Taiwan: **visa required**.
  - China: 30 days visa-free only **through Dec 31 2026**. Not on the 240h transit list, only 24h transit.
  - Hong Kong and Philippines: visa-free per third-party sources.
  - Thailand: rules changing; assume a visa.
- **Oceania:**
  - New Zealand: visa waiver + NZeTA.
  - Australia: Uruguay appears on the transit-without-visa list (airside, under 8h). Otherwise a free subclass 771 transit visa.
- **Europe:**
  - Schengen: visa-free. ETIAS not live yet; check travel-europe.europa.eu.
  - UK: airside transit ETA-exempt (temporary). Leaving the airport needs an ETA.
  - Qatar and Ethiopia: airside transit with no visa.

## Pricing quirks
- **Point of sale:** for flights starting in the US, use the airline's US site. LATAM's UY site was 41% pricier for JFK→MVD.
- **Pay in USD** with a card that has no foreign-transaction surcharge. Argentine cards may carry extra taxes or "percepciones" on foreign-currency purchases depending on current rules. Ask the user and don't assume.
- **Itaú LATAM Pass Visa Infinite (UY):** gives LATAM Gold, an extra 23 kg bag for the cardholder on LATAM-operated flights (the LATAM Pass number must be on the booking), seat selection, Santiago lounge + companion, upgrade requests, and Visa lounge passes. Factor these in when comparing LATAM fares.
- **Fares are quoted in USD on most UY/AR airline sites.** Buquebus quotes UYU. Convert with a live rate (`open.er-api.com`).

## Customs (if the trip includes shopping)
- Uruguay: duty-free allowance by air is about US$500. Brazil: US$1,000 by air, with a laptop counting toward it. Over the limit there's a 50% tax, plus a fine if undeclared. Check current rules.
